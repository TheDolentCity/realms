House Ulfrar is a Great House of Westmark and act as Wardens of Barrowdell. They are distrusted by all, but none more so than House Ironwood. They have raided Frostwatch through the guise of outlaws for hundreds of years. The two houses are perpetually on the brink of all out war, leading to House Ulfrar maintaining a strong and reliable seat of power.

## Realm | Barrowdell

Barrowdell is a central region with a cold and wet climate. House Ulfrar has a long history of attacking neighboring realms or orchestrating raids that are later denounced for appearances sake. The realm is filled with mercenaries who believe [The Cairn Eternal](/setting/religion#the-cairn-eternal) has bestowed them with the divine right to pillage other realms. Thieves, highwaymen, and other criminals are forced into the army under threat of death.

## Regent | Unnamed

Regent Ulfrar is the Warden of the Barrowdell realm. The reputation of their ancestors precedes them: few trust an Ulfrar noble. However, their natural charisma, strength, and martial prowess cannot be overlooked.
