House Ulfrar is a Great House of Westmark and act as Wardens of [Barrowdell](/realms/barrowdell). They are distrusted by all, but none more so than House Ironwood. They have raided Frostwatch through the guise of outlaws for hundreds of years. The two houses are perpetually on the brink of all out war, leading to House Ulfrar maintaining a strong and reliable seat of power.

## Regent | Unnamed

Regent Ulfrar is the Warden of the Barrowdell realm. The reputation of their ancestors precedes them: few trust an Ulfrar noble. However, their natural charisma, strength, and martial prowess cannot be overlooked.
