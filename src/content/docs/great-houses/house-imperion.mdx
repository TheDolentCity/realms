House Imperion is a Great House of Westmark and act as Wardens of [Fairfield](/realms/fairfield). King Cassandra was a member of House Imperion and responsible for the unification of Westmark. House Imperion protects her legacy and has produced several strong kings after her. Though many grow weary of House Imperion's apparent alliance with House Langloom.
