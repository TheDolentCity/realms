House Ironwood is a Great House of Westmark and act as Wardens of [Frostwatch](/realms/frostwatch). They rule over this vast region and are one of the oldest lineages. Their seat of power, Remheim, was one of the only castles that did not fall during Eastmark's invasion.

- **Coat of Arms**: An ironwood tree and a harpoon head on a white banner.
- **Religion**: [The Great Beasts](/setting/religion#the-great-beasts)
- **Heir**: TBD

## Regent | Unnamed

Regent Ironwood is the Warden of the Frostwatch realm. Ironwood Nobles are regarded as honorable and pragmatic leaders. Trained in swordcraft, tactics, and history from a young age, they'll do whatever it takes to feed their people.
