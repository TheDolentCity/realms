House Moxton is a Great House of Westmark and act as Wardens of Greyspire. These central mountains are covered in snow and provide strong fortification. The land and people are both distant and independent. House Moxton's seat of power, the Crag, was carved directly into the mountain itself and is nearly impregnable.

## Regent | Unnamed

Regent Moxton is the Warden of the Greyspire realm. House Moxton is generally regarded as isolationist, proud, and temperamental. They are naturally sturdy and do not balk at the thought of war. To each Moxton Regent, they are the King of the Crag.
