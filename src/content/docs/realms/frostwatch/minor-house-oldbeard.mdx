## 🔹 Angvir Oldbeard
> _Head of House Oldbeard — Vassal to House Ironwood — The Knotted One_ <br/>
> (he/him) — 84 years old

He is an old man, experienced in battle. Most of his body is covered in thick, knotted scars earning him the nickname the "Knotted One". He was mentor to Baeloc Ironwood as he aged. His husband is dead, but he has four sons who will outlive him. They often fight and bicker; many are concerned one of the younger sons may try to usurp their father's position. 

- <Caption text="Experienced in battle" variant="success" />
- <Caption text="Covered in thick knotted scars" />

## 🔸 Erik Oldbeard
> _Heir to House Oldbeard — First in Line_ <br/>
> (he/him) — 60 years old

Long-groomed for his father's position, Erik values responsibility and honesty. The people agree he provides both. It helps that he looks like the leader of a great house with his tall and noble looks. 

- <Caption text="Responsible" variant="success" />
- <Caption text="Noble looks" />

## 🔸 Leif Oldbeard
> _Heir to House Oldbeard — Second in Line_ <br/>
> (he/him) — 58 years old

Leif always preferred a more lively lifestyle, traveling on the road with minstrels and puppeteers rather than taking any of his duties seriously. He can be crass, bawdy, and outright rude to other nobles. His father has tried to stop him from drinking for years.
