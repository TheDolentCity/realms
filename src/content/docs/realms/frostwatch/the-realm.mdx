## Geography
Frostwatch is the most northern realm, and one of the largest too. To the West and North it borders the sea. The realm of Barrowdell can be found where its southernmost hills turn marshy and the Great Lake is considered the eastern border of Misthaven. The realm is made up of vast moorlands, dense forests, and frozen hills. Despite its size, the region is not as populated as more plentiful southern realms. Most villages are located along the coastline, leaving the expansive forests and moorlands largely undeveloped.

- <Caption text="Lower population" variant="danger" />

## People
This is a cold northern land. The people who live here may seem bitter to outsiders, but many of them have found peace. The six month winters and constant cold harden folk. They are used to extreme hardship. Among nobility, the toughness of these northern folk appears almost like excessive pride.

- <Caption text="Folk are used to extreme hardship" variant="success" />
- <Caption text="Considered too proud by nobles" variant="danger" />

## Economy
The conditions are harsh and make it difficult to grow food, but plenty of natural resources draw in traders from the South. However, many resort to theft and robbery in order to obtain food. Fishing is a crucial industry to keep people fed, and the most profitable form is whaling. Almost all whale oil in Westmark comes from the whalers of Frostwatch.

## Religion
The Sundered God is the most dominant religion across Westmark, but few followers of that faith can be found in Frostwatch. The people here believe in the older faiths. The most common faith is that of [The Great Beasts](/religion#the-great-beasts). There are few rules regarding worship of this folk religion, but many folk practice meditation in the wild. They treat their observance of natural events as good or ill omens. House Ironwood even has an official seer who they consult with such matters.

- <Caption text="Commoners and nobles are obsessed with omens" />

## Locations

#### Castle Remheim
The seat of House Ironwood. Its walls are massive, second only to Dawnstone, the castle of the King. It has many layers of defense, requiring attackers to breach several layers to reach the keep. As Dawnstone was not built prior to the invasion of Eastmark, Remheim was the only castle that was never breached by the invaders. Coincidentally, it was here that King Cassandra fled with other great house leaders and formed the Realm of Westmark.

- <Caption text="A massive castle, second only to Dawnstone, with many layers of defense" variant="success" />
- <Caption text="Its mighty walls were never breached during the Eastmark Invasion" variant="success" />

#### Oldbeard Hall
The seat of House Oldbeard. Warm and filled with drink and laughter, even in the dead of winter. Oldbeard Hall shorns most adornment. Instead, the hall is filled with warm pelts, long coal fireplaces, and wooden chairs made before the Eastmark Invasion. It is a relatively small burg, fit more for a hunting party than a noble retinue.
