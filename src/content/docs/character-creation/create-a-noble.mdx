## Age

Roll 1d6. This determines the noble's disposition, skills, and experience captions.

| 1d6 | Age                  | Captions                                              |
| --- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Child (8-12)         | None                                                  |
| 2   | Youth (14-20)        | 1 disposition caption                                 |
| 3   | Young Adult (22-28)  | 1 skill caption                                       |
| 4   | Mature Adult (30-46) | 2 skill captions                                      |
| 5   | Old (62-70)          | 1 experience caption                                  |
| 6   | Elder (86-99)        | 1 experience caption + 1 penalty disposition captions |

## Relationships

Roll 2d6. One for kinship, and one for the bond that they have with the leader of their house.

| 1d6 | Kinship           | Bond                           |
| --- | ----------------- | ------------------------------ |
| 1   | Aunt or Uncle     | They would die for you         |
| 2   | Sister or Brother | They will do whatever you ask  |
| 3   | Daughter or Son   | They want to protect you       |
| 4   | Niece or Nephew   | They look up to you            |
| 5   | Distant Cousin    | They envy you or your position |
| 6   | Fictive kinship   | They seek revenge against you  |
