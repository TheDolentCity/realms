This is a brief overview of the game rules. See each linked pages for more detailed information.

## Goal

The goal of the game is to achieve your character's personal objectives and also to create a complex, exciting narrative for everyone else playing the game.

## Player Characters

Players control the leader of a great or minor house. The leaders of great houses are **regents** and can perform actions that affect their realms. The leaders of minor houses are **vassals** that serve the regent of their realm.

## Structure

- During a turn, each regent can [perform a single action](/rules/actions), which they send to the Game Master in private.
- During a turn, any player can [write and send a letters](/rules/writing-letters) to other characters.
- During a turn, the Game Master rolls for each realm to determine if a random event occurs.
- At the end of each turn, the Game Master posts a [realm update](/home/realm-updates) that details action results and events.
- At the start of each turn, each player receives letters sent to them (unless they were intercepted).

## Turns

Each turn lasts a week, starting on Monday (East US) and ending at midnight on Sunday (East US). This turn also represents a week of in-world time.

## Actions

An action is specific, performed by a player character, and sent to the Game Master in private.

- **Action:** What are you doing?
- **Purpose:** What is your desired outcome?
- **Leverage:** Why should this action succeed? What [captions](/rules/captions) do you have to justify it?

If an action is unopposed then it occurs as planned. If the action faces opposition from another character or an established part of the narrative, the Game Master resolves the dispute with world logic. If the world logic still cannot resolve the conflict, then the Game Master uses a dice system and random roll to determine the outcome.

You can find [example actions here](/rules/actions).

## [Realm Updates](/home/realm-updates)

Once the Game Master has received all the actions from players, they craft a realm update. When the realm update is posted a new turn begins. Almost everything in the realm update is public and includes the following information:

- New [realm events](/rules/realm-events). One is rolled for each realm every update, but usually has no result.
- Regent [realm actions](/rules/actions) and their outcomes. Actions can fail and some may not even be public.
- Updates to character and realm [captions](/rules/captions) are public but not explicitly listed in the update.

## [Writing Letters](/rules/writing-letters)

Regents can write letters to any other regent or vassal, whether they're controlled by a player or not. These letters are freeform, there are _no_ rules about what can or cannot be in a letter. You do not even have to send any letters on your turn if you don't want to. This is the **only way** to communicate with other player characters.
