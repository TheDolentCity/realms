---
title: Writing Letters
description: Rules and suggestions for writing letters to other characters.
---

Regents can write letters to any other regent or vassal, whether they're controlled by a player or not. These letters are freeform, there are _no_ rules about what can or cannot be in a letter. You do not need to send letters on your turn if you don't want to, but they are the only way that you can communicate with distant characters.

## Rules you must follow

- Send your letters to the Game Master, **never** to another player.
- Send your letters before the end of the turn.

## Consent

Consent is important for anything romantic, sexual, or otherwise NSFW. Do not write anything in a letter or perform any action that would make someone in real life uncomfortable without first explicitly getting their approval. The GM will always read a letter first and if the content is too strong then they may ask you to rewrite it. If either party is uncomfortable with something that occurs please speak to the GM.
