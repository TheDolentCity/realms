---
title: Actions
description: A list of the various actions a regent can take in the game.
---

Regents can perform one action on their turn. When they do so, they can provide up to three reasons why it should succeed. Providing a caption counts as one reason. There are no limits on what these actions can be, but here are some ideas:

## Make a Decree

This is any new general decree or rule for the realm you rule over. It can affect almost anything about your realm. Here are a few examples:

- Impose new taxes to increase wealth, but this may decrease morale and loyalty.
- Throw a festival or tournament to increase spirits, at the cost of coin.
- Hire more guards to protect the roads from highwaymen.

## Invoke Religious Doctrine

Sway the opinion of your realm by invoking religious doctrine. Here are a few examples:

- In a realm where the Great Beasts are worshipped, you could state there has been an omen and the realm must march to war.
- In a realm that worships the Sundered God, you could force a disgraced heir to join a monastery and renounce their claim.

## Host a Council

Invite any number of regents or nobles to your realm for a council. Those who agree to visit can bring a retinue of no more than ten unless they wish to spend resources and actions to attend. If an invited guest is significantly far away, they may need to spend several actions to attend the council.

Out of game, when a council occurs the players and GM could schedule a time to have a voice call in Discord. Anything discussed in this voice call cannot be spied upon by any other character.

## Attend a Council

Most of the time when you attend a council, there is no required action. But if you must travel a significant distance, the GM may require you to spend actions or resources to achieve this.

## Court a Partner

Attempt to woo an NPC. A wooed NPC may be infatuated with you, but still not wish to marry you or abandon something more important to them. There may be a test of their honor or loyalty to determine their next steps.

You may choose to woo someone who is not of a great or minor house, in which case the result is almost always marriage.

## Construction

Propose a large construction project, such as a road, a castle, or several ships. This may improve roads, realm defenses, or fishing vessels, but will always cost a significant amount of resources and may cost significant time as well.

## Train

Spend significant time working to improve yourself. This may grant a new caption or improve an existing caption.

## Trade

Engage in diplomacy and trade with existing trade routes. May increase your food, resources, and wealth.

## Assassination

Plan an assassination. Based on the plan, the GM will assign the chance of success. If you are not thorough or have little to no plan, then not only will the assassination fail, but everyone will know you were the one who performed the assassination.

## Spy

Choose a regent, vassal, or location to send spies after. If successful, in addition to basic observations, you may intercept and read any letters from that location or individual. If you wish, you may destroy them or pass them along.

## Undermine

Choose a realm you would like to influence. This could mean popularizing unholy speeches, increasing crime with smuggled goods, or anything else you can imagine to sabotage the realm.

## Muster Troops

Muster troops for war. This action must be taken before the battle, pillage, or siege action can be performed. Mustering troops will decrease morale, food, resources, trade, and wealth.

## Disband Troops

Disband troops and send them home. This action can only be taken after mustering troops. Disbanding troops will increase morale, food, resources, trade, and wealth.

## Battle

You usually have to muster troops to take this action. Engage another army, navy, or foe in warfare. Both sides must provide the GM with their battle strategy and then the GM will make whatever checks they think necessary to perform the battle and determine the outcome.

Only the side that initiates the battle must spend an action on it.

## Pillage

You usually have to muster troops to take this action. Engage civilians with your army or navy. This is devastating for the realm you attack, but will impact your honor.

## Siege

You usually have to muster troops to take this action. Engage a fortress or castle in a siege. This may transition into a battle if either side wishes to, otherwise the action has no immediate outcome. The realm that is sieged quickly loses food, health, and morale until the siege ends.
